# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in `fieldbird-sim` respectful,
welcoming, and free from harassment for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific when discussing code or scientific assumptions.
- Welcome questions and different levels of programming or ecological experience.
- Focus criticism on ideas, evidence, and reproducible behavior rather than people.
- Acknowledge uncertainty, sources, and contributions honestly.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private data or correspondence.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, insults, or sustained personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Deliberate intimidation, disruption, or disclosure of private information.
- Knowingly misleading scientific claims, fabricated results, or misrepresented authorship.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests,
reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to Robin Kurth at
`robin.kurth24@gmail.com`. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details.
Reports will be reviewed promptly, handled as confidentially as practical, and
assessed in context. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when needed
to protect the project community.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation. False or malicious reports may
themselves be treated as a violation of this Code of Conduct.
